MS Dhoni - The Heart of Chennai Super Kings

Mahendra Singh Dhoni, lovingly known as "Thala", has been the face of CSK since the inception of the IPL. Under his leadership, CSK has lifted the IPL trophy five times — in 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, and 2023.

Known for his cool head, tactical genius, and incredible finishing ability, Dhoni continues to inspire fans and teammates alike. His presence on and off the field is unmatched, making him one of the most respected and successful captains in cricket history.

Even in the 2024 season, he shows no signs of slowing down — guiding the team with the same passion and commitment that has defined his glorious career. Beyond IPL, Dhoni led India to historic triumphs in the 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, and 2013 Champions Trophy.
